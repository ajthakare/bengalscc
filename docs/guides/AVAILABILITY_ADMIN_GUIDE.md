## Accessing the Portal

1. Log in to the **GSCC Admin Panel**
2. Click on **"Fixtures & Availability"** from the main menu
3. You'll see a list of upcoming fixtures organized by status

**Status Badges:**
- 🟢 **Selection Complete** - 11+ players selected, ready to announce
- 🟡 **Selection Pending** - Less than 11 players selected
- ⚪ **Available** - Fixture exists, players can mark availability

## Selecting Players for Matches

### Step 1: Open the Availability Modal

Click on any fixture card to open the availability management modal.

You'll see:
- **Total Players** - All players in the team roster
- **Available** - Players who marked themselves as available
- **Selected** - Players you've selected for the match (should be 11)
- **Availability Rate** - Percentage of players who responded

### Step 2: Review Player Information

Each player row shows:
- **Player Name** with badges:
  - 🔴 **VICE CAPTAIN** - Team vice-captain
  - 🟢 **CORE** - Core team member
- **Available?** ✅ - Player marked themselves as available
- **Selected?** ☑️ - Check this box to select player for the match
- **Match Duties** - Click to assign duties (see next section)

### Step 3: Select 11 Players

1. Review who marked themselves as available (blue checkmark)
2. Check the **"Selected?"** box for players you want in the playing XI
3. Select exactly **11 players** for the match
4. The stats at the top will update in real-time

### Step 4: Save Your Changes

⚠️ **CRITICAL:** Always click the **"Save Changes"** button at the bottom before closing the modal!

- Changes are NOT automatically saved
- If you close without saving, selections will be lost

## Assigning Match Duties

Match duties help organize team responsibilities during the game.

### Available Duties:
- ⚾ **Warm Up** - Lead warm-up session
- 💧 **Water Assignment** - Manage water/drinks
- 📊 **Scoring** - Keep score or assist scorer
- 🎒 **Team Kit** - Responsible for team equipment
- 🧊 **Ice** - Bring ice for injuries/cooling
- 🏟️ **Ground Set-up** - Arrive early to set up
- 🧹 **Ground Cleaning** - Clean up after match
- 📹 **Video Broadcast** - Record/stream the match

### How to Assign:

1. **Only selected players** can be assigned duties
2. Click on the **"Match Duties"** column for a selected player
3. A dropdown will appear showing all available duties
4. Check the boxes for duties you want to assign
5. Each duty can only be assigned to **one player** at a time
6. Duties already assigned to others will show "(Assigned to PlayerName)"
7. Click outside the dropdown or the ✕ button to close
8. Click **"Save Changes"** to persist the assignments

## Adding Match Links

You can add optional links to enhance the match experience:

### 1. YouTube Video Link 📹

- Scroll down in the modal to find **"YouTube Video Link"** section
- Paste the full YouTube URL (e.g., `https://youtube.com/watch?v=...`)
- This could be:
  - Live stream link (shared before match)
  - Match recording (shared after match)
- The link will appear on the public fixtures page

### 2. NCCA CricHeroes Link 🏏

- Find the **"NCCA CricHeroes Link"** section below YouTube
- Paste the CricHeroes scoring app URL (e.g., `https://cricheroes.com/...`)
- This allows fans to follow live scoring
- The link will appear on the public fixtures page

### Saving Links:

After adding links, click **"Save Changes"** button to update the fixture.

## Generating Team Announcements

Once you've selected 11 players and are ready to announce the team:

### Step 1: Verify Selection

- Ensure **exactly 11 players** are selected
- The fixture badge will change to **"11 Players Selected"** (green)
- Match duties should be assigned (optional but recommended)

### Step 2: Generate Announcement

1. Click the **"📱 Generate Announcement"** button
2. A modal will popup with the formatted team announcement

**The announcement includes:**
- Team name and season
- Match details (opponent, date, time, venue)
- Home/Away designation
- Ground address
- List of all 11 selected players with @ mentions
- Match duties assignments

### Step 3: Copy to Clipboard

1. Review the generated announcement
2. Click **"📋 Copy to Clipboard"** button at the bottom
3. You'll see a confirmation that the text was copied

### Step 4: Post to WhatsApp

1. Open WhatsApp and navigate to your team group
2. Paste the announcement (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V)
3. **IMPORTANT:** Replace @ mentions with actual WhatsApp contacts
   - The announcement shows `@PlayerName`
   - Replace with the player's actual WhatsApp @ mention
   - Example: Change `@Ajinkya Thakare` to the actual contact from your phone
4. Review and send the message

**Why replace names?**
- Player names in the system may not match WhatsApp contact names
- Proper @ mentions ensure players get notified
- This guarantees everyone sees the announcement

## Recording Match Results

**After the match is completed**, record the results for statistics and records:

### Step 1: Open the Match Modal

Click on the completed fixture to open the availability modal.

### Step 2: Scroll to Match Result Section

Near the bottom, you'll find the **"Match Result"** section.

### Step 3: Select Result

Choose from the dropdown:
- **Win** - Your team won
- **Loss** - Your team lost
- **Tie** - Match ended in a tie
- **No Result** - Match abandoned/no result

### Step 4: Select Player of the Match

From the dropdown, choose the player who performed best:
- Only shows players who were **selected** for this match
- This feeds into Player of Match statistics

### Step 5: Umpire Fee Details (if applicable)

If your team paid umpire fees:

1. **Paid Umpire Fee?** - Select **"Yes"**
2. **Paid By** - Select which player paid the umpire
3. **Amount Paid ($)** - Enter the amount (e.g., 35)

This helps track umpire fee expenses and who paid.

### Step 6: Save Changes

Click **"Save Changes"** to record all match results.

## Important Reminders

### ✅ Always Save Your Changes!

This is the **#1 mistake** admins make:
- Selections, duties, links, and results are NOT auto-saved
- You MUST click **"Save Changes"** before closing any modal
- If unsure, click Save again—it won't hurt!

### 📊 Check Player Badges

Pay attention to player role badges:
- **VICE CAPTAIN** (red) - Team leadership
- **CORE** (green) - Core team members with priority

These help you make informed selection decisions.

### 🎯 11 Players Required

- A cricket team needs exactly **11 players**
- The system shows how many are selected in real-time
- Fixture badge turns green when 11 are selected

### 📱 WhatsApp @ Mentions

When posting announcements:
- **DO NOT** just paste and send
- Replace `@PlayerName` with actual WhatsApp contacts
- This ensures players receive notifications

### 🏏 Match Links are Optional

- YouTube and CricHeroes links are not required
- Add them if available to enhance fan experience
- You can add/update links anytime before or after the match

### 📈 Results Impact Statistics

Recording match results affects:
- Team win/loss records
- Player of Match leaderboard
- Umpire fee tracking
- Season statistics

Take a moment to enter accurate information after each match.

## Quick Workflow Summary

**Before Match (2-3 days prior):**
1. Open fixture in Availability portal
2. Review who marked themselves available
3. Select 11 players for the match
4. Assign match duties to players
5. Add YouTube/CricHeroes links if available
6. **Save Changes**
7. Click "Generate Announcement"
8. Copy to clipboard and post to WhatsApp
9. Replace @ mentions with actual contacts

**After Match (same day or next day):**
1. Open the same fixture
2. Scroll to Match Result section
3. Select Win/Loss/Tie/No Result
4. Choose Player of the Match
5. Enter umpire fee details if applicable
6. **Save Changes**
