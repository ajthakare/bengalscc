How to Install the GSCC App

Add Golden State Cricket Club to your home screen — no App Store needed. Works on any iPhone.

Safari
1

Tap Share

Tap here →
3

Tap Add

1

Tap ↑

3

The GSCC icon appears on your home screen. Tap it to launch the app.
